Project overview

One place for staff operations, approvals, payroll visibility, and employee training.

Arsenal Staff Commander is a public, noindex demo for a fictional company called Arsenal Service Group. It shows how a custom internal business application can bring staff profiles, work submissions, bulk approvals, payroll visibility, training certifications, supervisor sign-off, administrator approval, reports, and connected learning progress into one command center.

Demo company

Arsenal Service Group

The demo uses fictional team members, example-demo.com email addresses, fake payroll amounts, and synthetic training records.

Product type

Operations and Training Command Center

A white-label internal dashboard that can be adapted for service businesses, contractors, facility teams, nonprofits, and event companies.

Business goal

Replace scattered tracking

Move work submissions, approvals, payroll visibility, and training status out of disconnected spreadsheets, email threads, and shared documents.

What the demo includes

A working noindex demo with role-based views and synthetic data.

Open Working Demo

Work submissions and bulk approvals

Submitted work includes team member, job type, date, start time, end time, status, amount, notes, created date, approved-by fields, selection checkboxes, approve selected, and approval history.

Payroll visibility and performance

Team payroll and performance views show fictional rates, hours or job counts, summary totals, missing-rate warnings, export simulations, and comparison bars.

Employee training and certifications

The training area includes a roster, progress filters, required and optional training, supervisor sign-off, administrator approval, certifications, and reports.

Build approach

Start with the workflow, then build the smallest command center that makes the work visible.

A real build would use separate hosting, separate database, separate environment variables, role-based access, audit logging, rate limiting, anti-bot protection, and controlled outbound email routes.

1. Workflow Review

Map how staff submissions, approvals, training, payroll visibility, and reports work today.

2. Command Center Map

Identify the roles, screens, statuses, data fields, reports, and edge cases that matter most.

3. Focused Build

Build the highest-value workflow first instead of forcing the team into generic software.
